## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the rigor of high-caliber literary journals paired with the definitive analytical power of modern decision science. It serves voracious, discerning readers, literary critics, book club curators, and academic researchers who demand nuanced evaluation over blunt star ratings.

The visual style blends refined editorial journalism with structural analytical minimalism:
- **Atmospheric Authority:** The interface communicates quiet gravitas through generous margins, deliberate baseline rhythms, and bookish materiality, eschewing flashy ornamentation in favor of intellectual restraint.
- **Critical Transparency:** Dense synthesis reports, trait alignments, and excerpted citations are treated with structural hierarchy reminiscent of peer-reviewed comparative reviews and forensic briefings.
- **Strict Analytical Separation:** The UI enforces an uncompromising distinction between objective public critical reception (the literary consensus) and subjective personal taste alignment (reader-specific resonance). These two operational pillars must never collapse into an aggregated or blended rating.

## Colors

The palette establishes an intellectual, tactile environment reminiscent of archival ink on heavy stock paper, augmented by clear, disciplined analytical markers.

### Core Roles
- **Primary (`#0F172A`)**: Deep archival slate navy. Used for core typography, authoritative mastheads, high-contrast structural framing, and primary navigational anchors.
- **Secondary (`#B45309`)**: Antique brass and burnt amber. Used for critical verdict accents, active highlights, analytical progress markers, and literary citations.
- **Tertiary (`#059669`)**: Scholarly emerald. Dedicated to positive evidentiary confidence, high reader-trait convergence, and rigorous consensus validation.
- **Neutral (`#64748B`)**: Weathered slate. Used for secondary metadata, citation footnotes, deactivated traits, and muted comparative lines.

### Surface System
- **Canvas Base (`#FAF9F6`)**: Warm unbleached parchment that reduces eye fatigue across long-form synthesis readings.
- **Elevated Canvas (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure parchment white for active analytical cards, deep-dive comparison panels, and reader match matrices.
- **Muted Substrate (`#F1F5F9`)**: Soft slate-tinted cream for structured data tables, evidence backdrops, and citation modules.

### Semantic Tones
- **Sufficient Evidence / Strong Convergence**: Emerald (`#059669`) with an archival mint surface tint (`#ECFDF5`).
- **Limited Evidence / Partial Alignment**: Amber (`#D97706`) with an aged papyrus tint (`#FFFBEB`).
- **Insufficient Evidence / Critical Friction**: Rose (`#E11D48`) with a wash tint (`#FFF1F2`).
- **Indeterminate / Unfactored Trait**: Muted Slate (`#64748B`) with an inert surface tint (`#F8FAFC`).

## Typography

Typography establishes an intentional cadence between literary contemplation and scientific inspection:

- **Editorial Serif (`Newsreader`)**: Reserved for literary expression—work titles, synthesized prose summaries, critical pull-quotes, and thematic chapter-style section headers. The optical styling references fine letterpress, commanding unhurried reading and authority.
- **Analytical Sans (`Inter`)**: Deployed for data density, scoring matrices, algorithmic convergence metrics, parameter labels, and evidentiary citation breakdowns. It ensures crystalline legibility in compact environments.
- **Case and Tracking Hierarchy**: Micro-labels, edition classifications, and confidence tiers utilize uppercase letterforms with explicit character spacing (`letter-spacing: 0.05em`) to evoke formal cataloging conventions.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is anchored in an editorial column structure that balances dense metadata matrices with open, book-like margins.

### Grid Construction
- **Desktop (1024px and above)**: A structured 12-column grid capped at a maximum width of `1280px`. The primary evaluation canvas implements a deliberate dual-column division:
  - Left Column (7 columns): Analytical Synthesis, Critical Excerpts, Public Consensus.
  - Right Column (5 columns): Personal Alignment Matrix, Trait Compatibility, Verdict Decision Tree.
- **Tablet (768px to 1023px)**: An 8-column layout with `1.5rem` gutters. Dual-pillar content transitions into stacked thematic sections with quick-jump rail navigation.
- **Mobile (below 768px)**: A 4-column fluid layout with `1rem` outer canvas margins. Tabulated switches decouple Public Consensus and Personal Alignment to protect screen real estate and prevent visual entanglement.

### Spacing Philosophy
Spacing operates on a strict 8px/4px underlying rhythmic scale. Generous vertical gaps (`space-xl`) delineate disparate evidentiary stages, while compact gaps (`space-xs` and `space-sm`) bind evidence tokens directly to their parent claims.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids theatrical drop shadows and synthetic neon glows, relying on fine editorial framing, material layering, and crisp borders.

- **Low-Contrast Framing**: Surfaces delineate boundaries primarily using 1px hairpins tinted in slate (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)` on parchment, `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.15)` on crisp white).
- **Substrate Tiers**:
  - `Level 0 (Canvas)`: Base warm parchment (`#FAF9F6`), recessive and non-reflective.
  - `Level 1 (Card/Report Section)`: Bright parchment (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px border (`#E2E8F0`). Used for isolated analysis blocks.
  - `Level 2 (Overlays & Critical Popovers)`: Grounded white with a subtle, tinted ambient spread (`box-shadow: 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`).
- **Analytical Insets**: Recessed containers for raw quotation digests and technical methodology notes use neutral-tinted fills (`#F8FAFC`) with hairline internal borders to signify an archival register.

## Shapes

The shape architecture is restrained and architectural, utilizing soft, minimal rounding (`roundedness: 1`, base `0.25rem`). This evokes fine stationery, book jackets, cut cards, and library index trays.

- **Interactive Elements**: Inputs, action buttons, and edition chips carry a precise `0.25rem` (4px) radius.
- **Cards & Data Modules**: Evaluation panels, confidence gauges, and synthesis summaries utilize `0.5rem` (8px) corners to distinguish major compositional blocks without feeling playful.
- **Status Indicators & Micro-Badges**: Small confidence dots and indicator gauges are rendered fully circular (`9999px`) to create clear geometry distinct from rectangular textual cards.

## Components

### Dual Verdict Pillar (Core Architectural Component)
The dual verdict pillar prevents the conflation of critical consensus and individual resonance:
- **Public Critical Reception Panel**: Framed in crisp slate lines with an intellectual archival feel. Features quantitative consensus metrics (e.g., Critical Weight, Synthesized Reception Curve) alongside qualitative summary paragraphs rendered in `Newsreader`.
- **Personal Preference Match Panel**: Framed in antique brass accents (`#B45309`). Displays personal trait alignment percentages, pacing compatibility, thematic flags, and prose-style affinity.
- **Rule of Separation**: These panels must never share an aggregated composite score. A visible vertical ruling or contextual demarcation reinforces that a critical masterpiece can be a personal mismatch, and vice versa.

### Evidence Confidence Gauges
- Displays certainty of consensus based on review sample size and depth of critical commentary.
- Rendered as a horizontal partitioned bar divided into 3 or 5 blocks:
  - **Sufficient**: Solid emerald fill (`#059669`) with authoritative label "SUFFICIENT EVIDENCE".
  - **Limited**: Warm amber fill (`#D97706`) with an amber warning mark "LIMITED EVIDENCE".
  - **Unverified/Conflicting**: Slate hatch pattern with "INCONCLUSIVE DATA".

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Confirm Decision, Run Verdict)**: Solid deep slate (`#0F172A`) with parchment text (`#FAF9F6`), crisp 4px border-radius, and medium sans-serif typography. Subtly deepens on hover with zero elevation lift.
- **Secondary Action (Inspect Citations, Adjust Traits)**: Parchment background with a 1px solid slate outline (`#CBD5E1`) and deep slate text. Transitions to muted slate background (`#F1F5F9`) on hover.
- **Tertiary Action (Footnotes, Read Method)**: Flat, underline-offset links in antique brass (`#B45309`) without container borders.

### Trait Chips & Edition Tags
- **Edition Tags (Format & Printing)**: Monospaced or tracked uppercase labels (`0.6875rem`), framed in hairline borders with neutral canvas fills (e.g., `HARDCOVER • 1ST ED`, `UNABRIDGED AUDIO`).
- **Trait Alignment Chips**:
  - Positive match: Soft emerald tint (`#ECFDF5`), emerald text, prefixed with a solid square glyph.
  - Friction match: Soft rose tint (`#FFF1F2`), rose text, prefixed with a slash glyph.
  - Inactive/Neutral: Muted slate tint (`#F8FAFC`), slate text.

### Step-by-Step Analysis Indicators
- A horizontal breadcrumb-style rail indicating analytical phases: `1. Source Aggregation → 2. Consensus Synthesis → 3. Trait Mapping → 4. Verdict Rendered`.
- Unfinished steps: Muted hairline circles with slate labels.
- Active step: Antique brass ring with a pulsating micro-dot.
- Verified step: Deep slate solid node with a clean checkmark.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Minimalist parchment-white inputs framed by clean 1px borders (`#CBD5E1`).
- Focus state: Replaces border with a 1.5px solid archival slate ring (`#0F172A`) with zero glowing halo.
- Placeholder text: Stylized in italicized `Newsreader` to evoke a quiet library inquiry prompt.
